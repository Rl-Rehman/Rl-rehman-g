Grand Theft Auto V (GTA V) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. Released in 2013, it has become one of the best-selling and most critically acclaimed video games of all time. Set in the sprawling fictional city of Los Santos, which is inspired by Los Angeles, the game follows three protagonists—Michael, Franklin, and Trevor—each with their unique backgrounds and motivations.
